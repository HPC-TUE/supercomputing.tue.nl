# Contact

Are you struggling with large computations on your laptop or workstation? Do you need to speed up your compute work?
Get in touch to find out how we can help.

While the SCC works primarily for clients within TU/e, assignments are also accepted from external research
institutions and industry.

## Meet the team

The Supercomputing Center is part of Library and Information Services (LIS) and based at EAISI in the Neuron building.
Not sure who to ask? Email [supercomputing@tue.nl](mailto:supercomputing@tue.nl) and we will connect you with the
right expert.
