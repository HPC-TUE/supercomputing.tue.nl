# TU/e Supercomputing Center

Computation has become an integral part of scientific research. Many computations can be performed on your laptop or
workstation, but more and more, large-scale computational work requires professional methods, tools and equipment.
{ .lead }

At the TU/e Supercomputing Center (SCC) we help you run large computations quickly and easily. We help you with
consulting, hands-on support and dispatch to appropriate infrastructures. This may be advice on what infrastructure
suits you best, hands-on help with optimizing your code, finding the right compute infrastructure for your workload, or
even simply getting bureaucratic or financial hurdles out of the way.

## Find the right platform

Research computing scales in steps. Start where your work fits today, and move up when you outgrow it. We help you
choose the right step and get access, from your own workstation to European supercomputers.

--8<-- "hpc-staircase.inc"

At TU/e, researchers can test and develop on [Umbrella](documentation/index.md) and [Spike-1](spike-1/index.md), then scale up to
[Snellius](https://www.surf.nl/en/services/snellius-the-national-supercomputer){:target=_blank} at SURF or to
[EuroHPC](https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/){:target=_blank} systems such as LUMI and Leonardo when your research needs it.
