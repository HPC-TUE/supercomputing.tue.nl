# NVIDIA GTC Viewing Party at TU/e

**On Monday 26 October, TU/e hosts the NVIDIA GTC Viewing Party in the Blauwe Zaal: a condensed recap of Jensen
Huang's GTC Berlin keynote, talks on AI in research and industry, and an afternoon of networking with AI
professionals from the Netherlands and Belgium. Admission is free; registration is required.**

Richard Zoontjens, lead of the TU/e Supercomputing Center, is one of the speakers:
*If you don't compute, you don't compete.*

<!-- more -->

##### Speakers

- **Fons van der Sommen** (TU/e): AI that helps doctors see cancer sooner
- **Richard Zoontjens** (TU/e Supercomputing Center): If you don't compute, you don't compete
- **Dr. Anneloes Smitsman** (EARTHwise): AI agents that play win-win
- **Igor Soltysik** (NVIDIA): the GTC keynote decoded
- **Georgio Mosis** (Wildlife Forensics Academy): AI against wildlife crime
- **Viktor Mirovic** (TasteIndex): your taste is data
- **Andrew Foe** (iOdis): NVIDIA DGX Spark giveaway. One DGX Spark is raffled among registered attendees who are
  present at the closing.

##### Practical information

- **When:** Monday 26 October 2026. Doors open at 14:30, the program runs from 15:00 to 18:45, followed by a buffet,
  drinks and networking until about 20:30.
- **Where:** Blauwe Zaal, Auditorium, Groene Loper 3, Eindhoven.
- **Language:** English.
- **Admission:** free, with registration. Seats are limited.

!!! warning "Can't make it after all?"

    Cancel in time via your confirmation email, so your seat can go to someone on the waiting list. The organisers
    invoice registered guests who do not attend without cancelling €99.

##### Organisers

The GTC Viewing Party is a joint initiative of iOdis, MDCS.AI, PNY, HP Inc., Red Hat, Equinix, Prodrive Technologies
and NVIDIA, hosted by TU Eindhoven. Registration is handled by the organisers; see the privacy statement on the
[event website](https://www.gtcviewingparty.com/){:target=_blank}. Agreeing to be contacted by the organisers is
optional on the registration form.
