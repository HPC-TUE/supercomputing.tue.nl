# Platforms

Which platform fits depends on your workload, your data and who you are. Below is an overview of the platforms we
support, from TU/e's own clusters to national and European supercomputers. Not sure where your work fits?
[Get in touch](../contact.md): we advise on the best platform, help with access and paperwork, and help you move up
when your research grows.

<div class="grid cards platforms quick-links--pairs" markdown>

-   :material-server:{ .lg } __Umbrella HPC Cluster__ <span class="platform-tag">TU/e</span>

    ---

    The TU/e HPC cluster, built from departmental CPU and GPU resources and managed by the Supercomputing Center
    Infra Team.

    - **For:** TU/e employees and students with a TU/e account. Generic nodes are open to everyone at TU/e;
      departmental partitions to their own groups.
    - **Best for:** batch jobs with Slurm, simulations, data analysis, and interactive work through Open OnDemand.
    - **Access:** [request an account](../documentation/steps/request/index.md).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Umbrella documentation](../documentation/index.md)

-   :material-chip:{ .lg } __Spike-1__ <span class="platform-tag">TU/e</span>

    ---

    NVIDIA DGX B200 platform for AI research: 4 nodes with 32 B200 GPUs, 250 TB DDN storage, Run:ai scheduling and
    hosted large language models.

    - **For:** TU/e researchers and students in the associated departments.
    - **Best for:** large-scale and distributed training, workloads that need a lot of GPU memory, and
      container-based AI workflows.
    - **Access:** application and intake with an SCC AI engineer.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: About Spike-1](../spike-1/index.md)

-   :material-server-network:{ .lg } __Snellius__ <span class="platform-tag platform-tag--national">National</span>

    ---

    The Dutch national supercomputer, hosted by SURF.

    - **For:** researchers at Dutch institutions.
    - **Best for:** workloads that outgrow TU/e systems.
    - **Access:** through SURF. We can help you prepare your application.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Snellius at SURF](https://www.surf.nl/en/services/snellius-the-national-supercomputer){:target=_blank}

-   :material-earth:{ .lg } __EuroHPC__ <span class="platform-tag platform-tag--national">European</span>

    ---

    Pan-European supercomputers such as LUMI and Leonardo.

    - **For:** researchers and organisations in Europe.
    - **Best for:** the largest simulations and AI workloads.
    - **Access:** through EuroHPC access calls. We can help you prepare your application.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: EuroHPC](https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/){:target=_blank}

</div>
